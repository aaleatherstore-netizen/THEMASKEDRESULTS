FAQ

Common Sawalaat

Jo aksar loog puchhtey hain — seedhe jawab.

Kya main Shopify ke baare mein kuch jaane baghair store banwa sakta hun? +
Bilkul! Aapko kuch jaanne ki zaroorat nahi. Hum shuru se aakhir tak sambhaltey hain aur delivery ke baad training bhi dete hain taake aap asaani se apna store manage kar sakein.
Payment kaisy karni hai — safe hai? +
Payment bilkul safe hai. Hum 50% advance lete hain — baaki 50% sirf tab jab aap store dekh kar khush ho jaayein. Bank transfer ya Easypaisa/JazzCash — jo aapko suit kare.
Kya store delivery ke baad koi changes karwaa sakta hun? +
Haan! Delivery ke baad hum free revisions dete hain. Agar koi cheez aapko pasand nahi toh hum change karengy — bina kisi extra charge ke.
Shopify ki monthly fee kaun dega? +
Shopify ka Basic plan $29/month hai jo aap khud Shopify ko directly pay karte hain. Hum sirf store banane ka charge lete hain — koi hidden fee nahi.
Pakistan mein Shopify se online payment ho sakti hai? +
Haan! Hum aapke store mein multiple payment methods setup karte hain — Cash on Delivery (most popular), bank transfer, aur Stripe bhi (if eligible). Aapka koi sale nahi rukeyga.
